Version 1.1.3 (8-10-26)

  • Hidden Whitelist: Added the option to whitelist certain apps by their Bundle-ID's to prevent them from hiding the dock when they're displayed, This gives you, the user, more freedom and personal preference. Instructions are easy to follow and are included in the Readme.md/Repo page.
